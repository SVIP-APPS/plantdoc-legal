FAQ

Is PlantDoc free?
Yes. There are no subscriptions or purchases. Each device gets 3 free AI requests per day (identify, diagnose, or ask the Plant Doctor), refreshing at midnight.

The identification looks wrong.
Take a clear, well-lit photo with the leaves or flowers filling most of the frame. Several angles help for diagnosis. Results are AI estimates, so check the confidence score.

I'm not getting reminders.
Allow notifications for PlantDoc in the iOS Settings app and make sure the reminder is switched on.

How do I delete my data?
Open the App > More > Delete All My Data. Everything is stored only on your iPhone.
