PlantDoc

Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains how PlantDoc ("we", "us") handles information when you use the PlantDoc iOS app (the "App"). The App has no accounts and no servers of ours: it works directly on your device and keeps as little data as possible.

Information processed to provide the App's features

  • Photos you take or choose. When you identify a plant or run a diagnosis, the photo(s) you select are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection directly from your device to Google's Gemini API (Google LLC) so the AI can analyze them. The result is returned to your device. We do not operate any server in between and never receive your photos.
  • Questions you ask the AI Plant Doctor. The text you type, and any photo you attach, is sent the same way to produce an answer. Recent messages from the current chat are included so the AI can follow the conversation.
  • Camera and photo library. The App uses the camera to take plant photos and the system photo picker to let you choose photos. It only receives the photos you select.

Google Gemini

Only after you agree in the App, your photos and questions are sent to Google's Gemini API. The request contains no name, email, location, or account information. Google may process and temporarily retain this content and, because the App uses Google's free tier, may use it to improve its products; see Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. Please do not include people, faces, or sensitive personal information in photos. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the App under More > Allow AI analysis.

Backup AI service (Groq)

If Gemini is temporarily unavailable, text-only questions to the AI Plant Doctor may be sent instead to Groq, Inc. (United States), which runs OpenAI's open-weight gpt-oss model. Photos are never sent to this backup service. The request contains no name, email, location, or account information. See Groq's Privacy Policy.

What is stored on your device

  • Your scan history, saved plants, photos, and care reminders are stored only on your device. You can delete everything in More > Delete All My Data, or by deleting the App.
  • Reminders are delivered as local notifications scheduled on your device. Notifications are optional.
  • To enforce the free daily allowance (3 AI requests per day), the App keeps a small daily counter in your device's keychain. It is never sent to us.

Tracking and advertising

The App contains no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not receive or keep personal data or your photos, there is generally nothing for us to access or delete. If you have a request or question, including under the CCPA or GDPR, contact us at 9934780@qq.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
